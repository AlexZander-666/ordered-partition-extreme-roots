# Correspondence with the ordered model

An ordered set partition of n labels contributes one factor of t for every block of cardinality at least j. The original row is linked to its large-block EGF by splitting an ordered list at every large block. For n=jd+r, its reciprocal variable is w=G d^j/t, where G=j!(1+1/(j+1))^j. Roots of least reciprocal modulus correspond to roots of greatest original modulus.

The root index is zero-based in Lean and one-based in the paper. The largest K+1 original moduli are attained by simple negative roots. Lower and upper rows of equal degree have the lower row's root first. When the degree increases, the upper row's root occurs first. The equal-residue comparison uses the nonzero limit of the first difference at distance j. All remaining complex roots lie strictly below the last selected modulus. The threshold and prefix length are fixed before the eventual row bound is chosen.

The author edition also treats the explicit first correction and the threshold-two trigonometric example.
